# FX screens on the desk (photos 04.11, fw 3.1) – notes so the photos need not be re-read
Layout everywhere: header (FX MIX knob, RESET, FX SPILL), faceplate picture in the middle, a bottom row of ~6 touch sliders with value.
Multi-module strips have tabs on the faceplate: EVEN/SOUL = GATE|EQ|DYN, VINTAGE = DYN|EQ|LVL, BUS = WARM|EQ|DYN, MASTER = TAPE|EQ|ENH|LIM.
Reverbs:
- HALL/ROOM/CHAMBER/PLATE/CONCERT/AMBI: white/cream 80s studio rack (Lexicon-like): blue LCD strip with 6 params + "LAYER 1", 6 vertical faders, red graph window, 3 small buttons. Bottom: PRE DLY SIZE DECAY BASS MULT DAMPING DIFFUSION (AMBI: TAIL GAIN).
- VSS3: silver panel, 6 faders, round preset dial with list (Acoustic Guitar Space, Medium Vocal Hall, Bright Theatre, Big Empty Club...), LOAD button. Bottom: DECAY LO DAMP HI DCY HI CUT REV LVL ER LVL.
- V-ROOM: dark 80s rack, red 7-segment displays (PRE DLY, DECAY, SIZE, DENSITY | LO MULT, HI MULT, ER DLY L/R, LO CUT, HI CUT, ER LEVEL, LEVEL), big centre knob, ADD/FREEZE.
- V-REV: black EMT-like: red tube on left, faders (Decay, Predelay, Lo Mult, Lo Cut, Hi Mult, Hi Cut, Modulation), LED ladders, green Output/Transformer buttons.
- V-PLATE: silver: FILTER slider, DECAY and PRE DELAY meters with blue knobs, red PLATE badge, COLOUR big knob (dark..bright), VU meter.
- BPLATE (Blue Plate): black/blue: two LCDs (DECAY s, DAMPING Hz), blue band with knobs Predelay Size Decay Damping BassMult Xover, buttons LoCut HiCut Mod Sprd.
- GATED / REVERSE: black "digital reverb": green LCD name, red 7-seg value, knobs PRE DELAY DECAY ATTACK(RISE) DENSITY SPREAD DIFFUSE, LO CUT, HIGH SHELF FREQ/GAIN.
- DEL/REV: black/blue "Dual FX": DELAY (time, feedback, level) and REVERB (decay, input, damp) blocks, LCDs, buttons Size PreDel Damp LoCut.
- SHIMMER: pedalboard on wood: purple Shimmer pedal, blue reverb pedal with LCD (pre delay, decay, size, damping), purple twin filter.
- SPRING: white tank face with green spring window, wood sides, knobs DECAY DENSITY BASS TREBLE.
Modulation/delay:
- DIMCRS: black rack: STEREO/MODE, OUTPUT meter, DRY/MIX, buttons OFF 1 2 3 4, POWER.
- CHORUS: pedals: blue Modulator (speed, wave, spread, phase), teal Chorus (delay L/R, depth L/R), twin filter.
- FLANGER: green feedback pedal (feed lc/hc, feedback), yellow Flanger (delay L/R, depth, speed, phase), twin filter.
- ST-DL: lime green "Delay" panel, big TIME and FEEDBACK knobs, factor/pattern/offset knobs, stereo feed mode diagram, hi/low cut, lamp.
- TAP-DL: black "UltraTap": orange width/diffusion, cream lo/hi cut, time 7-seg, factor, pre delay, repeats, jump/focus, slope, trim/balance graph.
- TAPE-DL: silver/green tape echo, two VU meters, TIME SUSTAIN | DRIVE FLUTTER.
- OILCAN: silver can face, wood sides, TIME SUSTAIN WOBBLE TONE.
- BBD-DL: purple pedal on wood with cables, FEEDBACK DELAY.
- ROTARY: wooden cabinet, top panel speed control slow/fast/stop, acceleration bass/horn, balance, distance, mix; grille slots.
- PHASER: blue Multi Phaser + LFO + Envelope (attack hold release graph).
- PANNER: silver Tremolo + LFO + Envelope.
- MOOD: blue multimode filter: envelope, filter (base freq, type, slope, resonance), drive, mix, LFO (depth speed phase wave), wood sides.
Pitch: PITCH black/blue LCD SEMI CENTS MS, mix/pitch/delay knobs, hi/lo cut. D-PITCH silver two sections A/B (delay pan semitones cents level) + hi/lo cut. PCORR dark: SPEED AMOUNT A4 PITCH + note keys C..B. DOUBLE: light panel, MODE knob (Tight Loose Group Detune Thick), MIX SPREAD. SUB: brown pedal OCTAVE 1/2 RANGE.

C5-CMB silver "Combinator" spectrum + coloured bands. LIMITER white precision limiter with meter. DE-S2 silver oval dual de-esser female/male, M/S.
